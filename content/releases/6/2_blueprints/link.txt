Title: Simpler blueprints

----

Subtitle: Less nesting, one concept: fields

----

Teaser:

Blueprints in Kirby 6 are a lot easier to handle: they need less nesting and fewer concepts. Define your fields once at the top of a blueprint and place them in tabs and columns by name.

Sections are gone: lists of pages and files are regular fields now and can be placed next to other fields with `width`, like everything else in your form. Your existing blueprints keep working, because Kirby converts their sections into fields automatically.

----

Example:

```yaml "/site/blueprints/pages/blog.yml"
title: Blog

fields:
  intro:
    type: textarea
  cover:
    type: filepicker
  articles:
    type: pagelist
    blueprints: article
    layout: table
    search: true

columns:
  - width: 2/3
    fields:
      - intro
      - articles
  - width: 1/3
    fields:
      - cover
```
