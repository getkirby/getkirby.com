Students can receive a free Kirby license upon verification of their educational email address or student ID card.

The license must *not* be used for anything other than personal and/or university/student projects.

<info>
Please send us an email from your school/college/university domain email address or attach a photo of your student ID card or enrollment certificate (one proof is enough). If you prefer to keep the license under a personal email address, feel free to add it. Otherwise we will assign the license to the email address you are writing from.
</info>
