Type: discounted

Text:

Non-profit organizations can apply for a free license or discount, depending on their business model.

Please let us know about the goals of the project and your funding situation (such as the rough yearly budget and sources of income). Without this information we will *not* be able to process your request.

<info>
Our good cause license program does *not* apply to religious organizations, churches or political parties. We also reserve the right to decline a discount in particular cases.

Our free or discounted licenses are aimed at organizations and projects that cannot afford purchasing a license. Please consider if the license fees pose a real burden for your project or if you could still support Kirby's active continued development by purchasing a license.
</info>
