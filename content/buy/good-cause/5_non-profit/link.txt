Type: discounted

Text:

Non-profit organizations can apply for a free license or discount, depending on their business model.

Please let us know about the goals of the project and your funding situation (such as the rough yearly budget and sources of income).

<info>
Our good cause license program does *not* apply to religious organizations, churches or political parties. We also reserve the right to decline a discount in particular cases.

Note that due to a high volume of discount requests we can only provide discounts to organizations with insufficient funding.
</info>
