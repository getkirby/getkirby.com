Title: Universities & educational organizations

----

Link: buy#good-cause

----

Type: discounted

----

Text:

We provide discounts for educational projects as well as selected internal projects and applications of educational organizations.

This applies to universities, schools and accredited educational institutions.

Research projects are eligible only if they lack sufficient funding and operate under Open Access rules (or equivalent). Please let us know about the project and your funding situation.

<info>
Note that we *cannot* provide discounts for general public sites such as marketing/PR/informational/operational websites of a school, university or faculty.
</info>
